## 1. Overview

Circles of Giving (COG) is a community ecosystem based in Safed, Israel, built on a single, powerful idea: **give what you can, receive what you need.** It is both a virtual platform and a physical community center, designed so that neighbors can help neighbors — sharing time, talent, and support without transaction, obligation, or judgment.

The platform is organized around four interconnected pillars:

| Pillar | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Core Social Interaction** | Connect, converse, and build relationships |
| **Community Operations & Support** | Find opportunities, attend events, respond to urgent needs |
| **Governance & Management** | Transparency, safety, accountability, and organizational health |
| **Foundational & Administrative** | The infrastructure that keeps everything running safely |

## 3. Key Platform Capabilities

### Community Connection
- **Community Feed** for stories, updates, and shared wins
- **Private messaging** between members
- **Member directory** with cause and location filtering
- **Volunteer profiles** with impact stats, badges, and hour logs

### Giving & Support
- **Opportunities board** to browse and express interest in giving opportunities
- **SOS Board** for urgent 24–48 hour response requests
- **Shabbat Meals** hosting and joining
- **Volunteer Events** with RSVP, calendar, and map views

### Workshops & Learning
- Workshop inquiry system for participation and leadership
- Category-based discovery (Arts & Crafts, Health & Wellness, Music, Cooking, Languages, and more)
- In-person, Zoom, and hybrid formats
- Accessibility options support

### Governance & Safety
- Admin panel for content, events, workshops, hours, and gallery management
- Moderation tools for community standards
- Trust & Safety commitments with privacy and verification
- Role-based access (admin, moderator, user)

### Impact & Analytics
- Volunteer hour logging and approval workflow
- Event attendance tracking
- Impact dashboards with monthly charts
- Badge system recognizing milestones (first volunteer, 50/100 hours, 5/10 events, community champion, and more)

### Corporate Engagement
- Corporate volunteering inquiry flow
- Team-building program coordination
- Employee-count and cause-interest intake

### Accessibility & Inclusion
- Bilingual support (English / Hebrew) with full RTL layout
- Accessibility options across events and workshops
- Mobile-first responsive design optimized for Android WebView
- Accessibility widget (high contrast, link highlighting, readable spacing, pause animations)

### Payments & Donations
- Secure donation flow via Payments by Wix
- Corporate partnership intake

## 5. The Circle Model

Every feature is designed around one idea: community flourishes when people freely give what they have and openly receive what they need — without transaction, without obligation, without judgment.
